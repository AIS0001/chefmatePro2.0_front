# ChefMate POS System - Complete Documentation

## System Overview
ChefMate is a comprehensive Point of Sale (POS) system designed for restaurants, cafes, and food service businesses. It provides complete restaurant management including inventory, sales, reporting, and customer management.

## Core Features

### 1. Dashboard & Analytics
- **Real-time Analytics**: Live sales data, revenue tracking, and performance metrics
- **Visual Charts**: Sales trends, top-selling items, and revenue analytics
- **Quick Access**: Direct access to key functions and recent activities

### 2. Master Data Management
- **Customer Management**: Add, edit, and manage customer information
- **Supplier Management**: Maintain supplier database with contact details
- **Table Management**: Configure dining tables for restaurant operations
- **Category Management**: Organize menu items by categories and subcategories
- **Payment Options**: Configure multiple payment methods (Cash, Card, UPI, etc.)

### 3. Inventory Management
- **Item Management**: Add new menu items with pricing and descriptions
- **Stock Management**: Track stock levels, add new stock, and manage inventory
- **Product Management**: Create and manage product variants
- **Stock Reports**: Generate inventory reports and low stock alerts
- **Automated Alerts**: Get notified when items are running low

### 4. Sales & POS
- **Point of Sale**: Complete POS interface for order taking and billing
- **Advance Orders**: Pre-order system for customers
- **Multiple Tax Systems**: Support for both GST and VAT tax calculations
- **Order Management**: Track orders from placement to completion
- **Bill Generation**: Professional invoice generation with company branding

### 5. Financial Management
- **Voucher System**: Receipt and payment vouchers for accounting
- **Expense Tracking**: Record and categorize business expenses
- **Tax Management**: Configure tax rates and calculate tax automatically
- **Financial Reports**: Comprehensive financial reporting

### 6. Reporting & Analytics
- **Sales Reports**: Daily, weekly, monthly sales analysis
- **Item-wise Reports**: Performance analysis by menu items
- **Customer Reports**: Customer purchase history and preferences
- **Supplier Reports**: Supplier transaction history
- **GST/VAT Reports**: Tax compliance reports
- **Low Stock Reports**: Inventory monitoring
- **Ledger Reports**: Customer and supplier account statements

### 7. User Management & Security
- **Role-based Access**: Admin, Cashier, Account user roles
- **User Profiles**: Individual user accounts with permissions
- **Profile Management**: Update user information and preferences
- **Security**: Secure login system with session management

### 8. System Settings
- **Core Settings**: Basic system configuration
- **Company Information**: Business details and branding
- **Tax Configuration**: Set up tax rates and types
- **Units Management**: Configure measurement units
- **Currency Settings**: Multi-currency support
